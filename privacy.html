Privacy Policy — Vi sao the Publisher

Effective date: October 24, 2026

  1. Data collected: after the account owner logs in, the tool receives only an access token and basic account info (display name, account ID), used solely to publish videos to that same account.
  2. Storage: tokens are stored locally on the channel owner's computer. They are never sent anywhere other than the official YouTube and TikTok APIs.
  3. Sharing: no data is sold, shared, or used for advertising or analytics.
  4. Other users: the tool does not collect data about viewers or any other users.
  5. Deletion: the owner can revoke access in Google or TikTok settings and delete the local token files at any time.
